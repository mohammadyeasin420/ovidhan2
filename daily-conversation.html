🗣️ Why Practice Conversations?

English is learned by using it – not by memorizing rules.

This page helps you practice the most common English conversations used in daily life. Whether you're speaking with a teacher, customer, immigration officer, doctor, colleague, or friend, these lessons teach practical English you can use immediately.

💡 Speaking Tips

  • Don't memorize – speak. Focus on understanding and using the language naturally.
  • Speak aloud. Even if you're alone, say the sentences out loud.
  • Listen several times. Repetition helps your brain learn the rhythm and sounds.
  • Repeat naturally. Try to copy the speaker's tone and speed.
  • Use the conversation in real life. The best practice is using what you've learned.
  • Practice every day. 10 minutes daily is better than 1 hour weekly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many conversations should I practice daily?
One complete conversation every day is enough to build consistent speaking confidence.
Should I translate every sentence?
Read the Bangla explanation first, then focus on understanding the English naturally rather than translating every word.
Is this useful for IELTS and job interviews?
Yes. These conversations build practical speaking skills useful for IELTS, interviews, travel, and everyday communication.
