❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many vocabulary words should I learn every day? ▾
Learning 5–10 words every day is enough to build a strong vocabulary over time. Consistency is more important than quantity.
Is this vocabulary free? ▾
Yes. Most vocabulary resources on Ovidhan are completely free. No sign‑up required.
Do words include Bangla meanings? ▾
Yes. Every word includes simple Bangla explanations to help Bangladeshi learners understand quickly.
Is pronunciation available? ▾
Yes. Every word includes pronunciation support with text‑to‑speech (requires internet).
Is this useful for IELTS? ▾
Yes. We have a dedicated IELTS vocabulary section with high‑frequency words and collocations.
Is this useful for BCS? ▾
Yes. We provide vocabulary specially selected for Bangladesh Civil Service Preliminary and Written exams.
Can children use this? ▾
Yes. We have a Kids Vocabulary collection designed for beginners with fun words and pictures.
What is the best way to learn vocabulary? ▾
The most effective method is spaced repetition with context. Learn words in sentences, practice with flashcards, and review them regularly. The Dakho app automates this process.
