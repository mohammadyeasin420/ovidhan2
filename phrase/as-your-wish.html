As Your Wish Meaning in Bengali

English expression

As your wish

বাংলা অর্থ

আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী / তোমার যেমন খুশি

এখানে speaker অন্যের পছন্দ মেনে নিচ্ছেন। বাংলায় ভদ্র বা চলতি রূপ পরিস্থিতি অনুযায়ী বদলায়।

What “As your wish” means

As your wish is often heard in South Asian English when someone accepts another person’s choice. In Bangla it usually means আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী or তোমার যেমন খুশি.

Learners should recognise the phrase because it appears in local conversation. In international standard English, As you wish, It's up to you, or Whatever you prefer often sounds more natural.

Meaning by context

  • 1. Accepting someone’s choice

    বাংলা · আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী

    Natural English · As you wish / Whatever you prefer

    We can leave now or after lunch. As you wish.

    এখনও যেতে পারি, দুপুরের খাবারের পরেও যেতে পারি। আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী।

  • 2. Leaving the decision to someone

    বাংলা · তোমার যেমন খুশি

    Natural English · It's up to you / You decide

    Tea or coffee? It's up to you.

    চা না কফি? তোমার যেমন খুশি।

  • 3. Agreeing, sometimes with soft reluctance

    বাংলা · তুমি যা চাও

    Natural English · If that's what you want / As you wish

    If that's what you want, we will change the plan.

    তুমি যা চাও, আমরা পরিকল্পনা বদলাব।

More natural English alternatives

Choose the English form that matches how freely you are handing over the choice:

  • As you wish — when you politely accept another person’s preference
  • It's up to you — when the other person should decide
  • Whatever you prefer — when several options are equally fine
  • If that's what you want — when you agree, sometimes with mild hesitation
  • You decide — in short, direct conversation

Example sentences

As your wish — we can book the morning train.

আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী — সকালের ট্রেন বুক করতে পারি।

Stay here or come with us. It's up to you.

এখানে থাকো বা আমাদের সঙ্গে এসো। তোমার যেমন খুশি।

Whatever you prefer is fine for the seating arrangement.

বসার ব্যবস্থায় আপনি যা পছন্দ করেন, সেটাই ঠিক আছে।

If that's what you want, I will send the file tonight.

তুমি যদি সেটাই চাও, আজ রাতেই ফাইলটা পাঠাব।

When learners may hear or use “As your wish”

In Bangladesh and nearby South Asian settings, As your wish can sound polite, formal, or slightly old-fashioned. Speakers use it to accept a choice without arguing.

With international listeners, exams, and careful writing, a clearer alternative usually carries the same meaning with less risk of sounding translated.

When another expression is more natural

  • For polite acceptance, prefer As you wish.
  • For everyday decisions among friends, prefer It's up to you.
  • When offering equal options, prefer Whatever you prefer.
  • Do not use this phrase when you still disagree strongly; say that clearly instead.

Common learner mistake

Do not treat As your wish as the only correct English form. Many speakers in South Asia use it, but As you wish is the standard word order.

Also check your tone. Said sharply, the same idea can sound cold or dismissive even when the Bangla meaning is soft.

Related expressions

  • wish — the base word behind the expression
  • prefer — useful when the idea is “যা পছন্দ করো”
  • decide — useful when you hand the choice to someone

FAQ

What is the Bangla meaning of “As your wish”?

সাধারণত আপনার ইচ্ছা অনুযায়ী বা তোমার যেমন খুশি বোঝায়।

Is “As your wish” wrong English?

It is widely understood in South Asian English. In international or formal use, As you wish or It's up to you is usually safer.

What is a safer alternative in careful English?

Use As you wish, It's up to you, or Whatever you prefer, according to the situation.

Does “As your wish” always sound polite?

No. Soft tone sounds cooperative. Sharp tone can sound unwilling or distant.
