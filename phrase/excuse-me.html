Excuse Me Meaning in Bengali

English expression

Excuse me

বাংলা অর্থ

মাফ করবেন / একটু শুনবেন / দুঃখিত / কী বললেন?

একই ইংরেজি বাক্য অনেক কাজে লাগে: মনোযোগ চাওয়া, পথ চাওয়া, বাধা দেওয়া, বা শুনতে না পাওয়া। বাংলা অর্থ context দেখে নিন।

What “Excuse me” means

Excuse me is a polite multi-purpose phrase. It can get someone’s attention, ask for space to pass, soften an interruption, or check what was said.

Because the jobs are different, Bangla changes: মাফ করবেন, একটু শুনবেন, দুঃখিত, or কী বললেন? Do not force one Bangla line onto every English use.

Meaning by context

  • 1. Getting someone's attention

    বাংলা · একটু শুনবেন

    Natural English · Excuse me / Pardon me

    Excuse me, is this the line for platform 3?

    একটু শুনবেন, এটা কি তিন নম্বর প্ল্যাটফর্মের লাইন?

  • 2. Asking someone to let you pass

    বাংলা · মাফ করবেন

    Natural English · Excuse me / May I please get through?

    Excuse me, may I please get through?

    মাফ করবেন, একটু পথ দেবেন কি?

  • 3. Interrupting politely

    বাংলা · দুঃখিত

    Natural English · Excuse me / Sorry to interrupt

    Excuse me for interrupting, but the guest has arrived.

    মাঝখানে কেটে দিয়ে দুঃখিত, কিন্তু অতিথি এসে গেছেন।

  • 4. Asking for repetition or showing surprise

    বাংলা · কী বললেন?

    Natural English · Pardon? / Sorry, could you repeat that?

    Excuse me? I did not catch the last price.

    কী বললেন? শেষ দামটা ধরতে পারিনি।

More natural English alternatives

Match the English form to the social job you need:

  • Excuse me — attention, path, polite interruption, or a soft check
  • Pardon me — a slightly more formal attention or apology cue
  • Sorry to interrupt — when you must break into a conversation
  • Could you repeat that? — when you missed the words
  • I'm sorry — when you need a clear apology for a mistake or harm

Example sentences

Excuse me, do you know where the ticket counter is?

একটু শুনবেন, টিকিট কাউন্টার কোথায় জানেন কি?

Excuse me, I need to step past your chair.

মাফ করবেন, আপনার চেয়ারের পাশ দিয়ে যেতে হবে।

Sorry to interrupt, but your phone is ringing.

মাঝখানে কেটে দিয়ে দুঃখিত, কিন্তু আপনার ফোন বাজছে।

Excuse me? Did you say the class starts at nine?

কী বললেন? ক্লাস কি নয়টায় শুরু?

When learners may hear or use “Excuse me”

Use Excuse me in crowded places, offices, classrooms, shops, and transport when you need a polite social signal.

Rising intonation can turn it into a request for repetition. Falling or firm tone more often means attention, path, or interruption.

When another expression is more natural

  • For a real apology after causing trouble, prefer I'm sorry.
  • If you only missed the words, Could you repeat that? is clearer than a bare Excuse me?
  • If you need urgent help, add the request: Excuse me, can you help me find gate 2?

Common learner mistake

Do not collapse every Excuse me into দুঃখিত. That Bangla word is closer to an apology, while Excuse me often only opens a polite request.

Also separate Excuse me from I'm sorry. One manages social space and attention; the other accepts fault or expresses regret.

Related expressions

  • excuse — the base verb/noun in the expression
  • sorry — useful when comparing I'm sorry
  • interrupt — useful for the interruption context
  • pass — useful when asking someone to make way
  • repeat — useful when you did not hear clearly
  • polite — useful for discussing tone and manners

FAQ

What is the Bangla meaning of “Excuse me”?

Context অনুযায়ী মাফ করবেন, একটু শুনবেন, দুঃখিত বা কী বললেন? হতে পারে।

Is “Excuse me” the same as “I'm sorry”?

Not always. Excuse me often manages attention or space. I'm sorry is stronger when you apologise for a mistake.

How do I ask someone to move in a crowded bus?

Say Excuse me or Excuse me, may I please get through?

What if I did not hear the person?

Use Excuse me? with rising tone, or say Sorry, could you repeat that?
