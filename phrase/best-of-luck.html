Best of Luck Meaning in Bengali

English expression

Best of luck

বাংলা অর্থ

শুভকামনা / তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা

কেউ পরীক্ষা, সাক্ষাৎকার বা নতুন কাজে যাওয়ার আগে শুভেচ্ছা জানালে এই অর্থ বোঝায়।

What “Best of luck” means

Best of luck is a warm wish for someone who is about to face a test, interview, competition, or new challenge. In Bangla it is usually শুভকামনা or তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা.

Good luck, All the best, and Wishing you the best are close alternatives. They are not always different in meaning; choose the one that fits the relationship and formality.

Meaning by context

  • 1. Before an exam or interview

    বাংলা · শুভকামনা

    Natural English · Good luck / Best of luck

    Your viva is tomorrow. Best of luck!

    কাল তোমার ভাইভা। শুভকামনা!

  • 2. Starting a job or project

    বাংলা · তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা

    Natural English · All the best / Wishing you the best

    All the best in your new teaching post.

    নতুন শিক্ষকতা পদে তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা।

  • 3. Before a competition or hard challenge

    বাংলা · সাফল্য কামনা করছি

    Natural English · Best of luck / Good luck

    The debate final starts at noon. Best of luck to your team.

    দ্বিপাক্ষিক বিতর্কের ফাইনাল দুপুরে। তোমাদের দলের সাফল্য কামনা করছি।

More natural English alternatives

These wishes are close in meaning. Use the one that matches tone and relationship:

  • Good luck — the most common short wish before a difficult moment
  • Best of luck — a warm, slightly fuller wish in speech or messages
  • All the best — common in farewell, transfer, or new-beginning contexts
  • Wishing you the best — a little more formal in cards, emails, or speeches

Example sentences

Best of luck with the BCS written exam.

বিসিএস লিখিত পরীক্ষায় শুভকামনা।

Good luck in your bank-job interview this morning.

আজ সকালের ব্যাংক চাকরির সাক্ষাৎকারে শুভকামনা।

All the best as you start the new research project.

নতুন গবেষণা প্রকল্প শুরু করতে তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা।

Wishing you the best in the football final.

ফুটবল ফাইনালে তোমার সাফল্য কামনা করছি।

When learners may hear or use “Best of luck”

Use Best of luck when someone is about to do something uncertain or demanding: an exam, interview, match, presentation, or first day at work.

It is friendly and positive. You can say it face to face, in a text, or at the end of a short note.

When another expression is more natural

  • For the shortest everyday wish, Good luck is enough.
  • For someone leaving a class, job, or city, All the best often fits well.
  • Do not use Best of luck for ordinary greetings such as hello or goodbye with no challenge ahead.

Common learner mistake

Do not invent a deep difference between Best of luck and Good luck when the situation is the same. Both can translate as শুভকামনা.

Avoid the word-for-word Bangla calque that sounds like a dictionary line. Prefer a natural wish such as শুভকামনা or তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা.

Related expressions

  • all the best — a closely related good-wish expression
  • luck — the key noun in the expression
  • best — useful when comparing Best of luck and All the best
  • success — useful when the Bangla idea is সাফল্য কামনা
  • exam — a common context for this wish

FAQ

What is the Bangla meaning of “Best of luck”?

সাধারণত শুভকামনা বা তোমার জন্য শুভকামনা বোঝায়।

Is “Best of luck” different from “Good luck”?

In many situations they are almost the same. Best of luck can sound a little fuller; Good luck is shorter and very common.

Can I use “Best of luck” before a job interview?

Yes. It is natural before interviews, exams, matches, and other challenges.

Should I say “Best of lucks”?

No. Keep luck uncountable here: Best of luck.
