📝 Grammar in Context

Reading Academy teaches grammar naturally through real sentences.

Past Simple

She read a book yesterday.

"read" is the past form of "read". The spelling is the same, but the pronunciation changes in the past tense (/red/). Past Simple is used for completed actions in the past.

🇧🇩 "read" এর present এবং past spelling একই, তবে past form-এর pronunciation /red/। অতীতের সম্পন্ন কাজ বোঝাতে Past Simple ব্যবহার করা হয়।

Adverbs of Frequency

She often drinks tea.

"often" shows how frequently an action happens. It normally comes before the main verb.

🇧🇩 "often" একটি কাজ কতবার ঘটে তা বোঝায়। এটি সাধারণত মূল verb-এর আগে বসে।

🧠 How to Practice Reading

👁️

1. Read First

Read the passage once without translating. Try to understand the general meaning.

🔍

2. Guess from Context

Look at surrounding words to guess the meaning of unfamiliar vocabulary.

🇧🇩

3. Check Bangla Meanings

Use the vocabulary list to confirm your guesses and learn new words.

🔄

4. Read Again

Read the passage a second time to see how the new words fit into the context.

🧪

5. Answer Questions

Test your comprehension. Review any mistakes.

📝

6. Review Mistakes

Understand why the correct answer is right and why your choice was wrong.
