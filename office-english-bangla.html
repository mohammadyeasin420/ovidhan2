📚 Essential Office Vocabulary

Learn the most common workplace words with Bangla meanings and examples.

Deadlineশেষ সময়সীমা

"The deadline for the report is Friday."

Meetingমিটিং / সভা

"We have a meeting at 10 a.m."

Scheduleসময়সূচী

"I'll check my schedule and let you know."

Taskকাজ / টাস্ক

"I have three tasks to complete today."

Updateঅপডেট / হালনাগাদ

"Can you give me an update on the project?"

Colleagueসহকর্মী

"My colleague helped me with the presentation."

Clientক্লায়েন্ট

"I have a meeting with a client today."

Projectপ্রকল্প

"We are working on a new project."

Availableউপলব্ধ

"I'm available at 3 p.m."

💬 Natural Office English

Bangla speakers often translate literally. Learn the natural professional alternatives.

একটু সময় দিতে পারবেন?

→ Could you give me a moment?

আমি আপনাকে পরে জানাচ্ছি।

→ I'll let you know later.

আমি কাজটা শেষ করেছি।

→ I've finished the task.

আমি বিষয়টা দেখে জানাচ্ছি।

→ I'll check and get back to you.

আমি কি আপনাকে কিছু জিজ্ঞেস করতে পারি?

→ Could I ask you something?

আমি আপনার সাথে একমত।

→ I agree with you.

🙏 Polite Office English

Learn the difference between direct and professional English.

Send me the file.

→ Could you send me the file, please?

"দয়া করে ফাইলটি পাঠাতে পারবেন?"
I don't understand.

→ Sorry, could you explain that again?

"দুঃখিত, আপনি কি আবার বুঝিয়ে বলতে পারবেন?"
I want a day off.

→ Could I request a day off?

"আমি কি একদিনের ছুটি চাইতে পারি?"
I can't do it.

→ I'm afraid I might not be able to complete it on time.

"আমি ভয় পাচ্ছি আমি সময়মতো শেষ করতে পারব না।"

📋 Giving Work Updates

Keep your manager and team informed with these practical phrases.

I've finished...আমি শেষ করেছি...

"I've finished the report."

I'm working on...আমি কাজ করছি...

"I'm working on the presentation."

I'll complete it by...আমি ... এর মধ্যে শেষ করব

"I'll complete it by Friday."

There's a small delay because...একটু দেরি হচ্ছে কারণ...

"There's a small delay because I'm waiting for approval."

I'll check and get back to you.আমি দেখে জানাচ্ছি।

"I'll check the numbers and get back to you."

⚠️ Common Bangladeshi Office English Mistakes

Fix these frequent workplace errors with Bangla explanations.

❌ I am agree with you.
✅ I agree with you.
🇧🇩 "Agree" এখানে verb, তাই "am" ব্যবহার করা হয় না।
❌ He go to office every day.
✅ He goes to the office every day.
🇧🇩 ৩য় পক্ষ একবচনে (He/She/It) verb-এ "s" যোগ হয়।
❌ Discuss about the project.
✅ Discuss the project.
🇧🇩 "Discuss" transitive verb, তাই "about" ছাড়াই object নেয়।
❌ I didn't went to the meeting.
✅ I didn't go to the meeting.
🇧🇩 "Didn't" এর পরে verb-এর base form (go) ব্যবহার হয়।
❌ I have done it yesterday.
✅ I did it yesterday.
🇧🇩 "Yesterday" অতীত সময় নির্দেশ করে, তাই Past Simple ব্যবহার হয়।
