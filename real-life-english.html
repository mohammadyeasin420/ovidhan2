Study tips for Bangla-speaking learners

  • Do not memorize long unnatural answers. Learn a short structure, then adapt it to your real life.
  • Listen once, read once, then speak aloud. Shadow the natural English lines.
  • Use Bangla explanations only when a phrase feels unclear — keep English as the main practice language.
  • Review common Bangladeshi learner mistakes so interview English sounds natural and confident.
