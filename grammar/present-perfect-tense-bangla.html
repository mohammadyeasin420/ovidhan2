Present Perfect Tense

CEFR: A2 ⏱ 8 min grammar

﻿# Present Perfect Tense

What is the Present Perfect Tense?

The Present Perfect Tense is used for past actions that have a result or connection to the present.

বাংলা:
Present Perfect Tense অতীতের এমন কাজ বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয় যার ফল এখনও বিদ্যমান।

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand when to use the Present Perfect
  • Use have/has + past participle
  • Talk about experiences and recent events

Formula

Positive

Subject + have/has + Past Participle
Example: She has finished her homework.

Negative

Subject + have/has + not + Past Participle
Example: They have not visited Dhaka.

Question

Have/Has + Subject + Past Participle?
Example: Have you seen this movie?

When Do We Use It?

  • ✅ Past experiences (e.g., I have been to Japan.)
  • ✅ Recent events (e.g., She has just left.)
  • ✅ Actions that started in the past and continue (e.g., I have lived here for 10 years.)

💡 Remember

Use have for I/You/We/They, and has for He/She/It.
Always use the past participle (verb 3).

Try It Yourself

  1. She (finish) her work.
  2. I (not/eat) lunch yet.
  3. They (go) to Cox's Bazar.
Show Answers1. She has finished her work. 2. I have not eaten lunch yet. 3. They have gone to Cox's Bazar.
