Past Continuous Tense

CEFR: A2 ⏱ 10 min grammar

﻿# Past Continuous Tense

What is the Past Continuous Tense?

The Past Continuous Tense is used for actions that were happening at a specific time in the past.

বাংলা:
Past Continuous Tense অতীতে কোনো নির্দিষ্ট সময়ে চলমান কাজ বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand when to use the Past Continuous
  • Form sentences with was/were + verb-ing
  • Talk about actions in progress at a past time

Formula

Positive

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing
Example: She was reading a book.

Negative

Subject + was/were + not + Verb-ing
Example: They were not playing football.

Question

Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?
Example: Were you watching TV?

When Do We Use It?

  • ✅ Actions in progress at a specific past time (e.g., I was sleeping at 10 pm.)
  • ✅ Background actions interrupted by another event (e.g., I was reading when the phone rang.)
  • ✅ Two simultaneous actions (e.g., She was cooking while he was watching TV.)

💡 Remember

Use was for I/He/She/It, and were for You/We/They.
Always use verb-ing.

Try It Yourself

  1. She (read) a book at 8 pm.
  2. They (not/sleep) when I called.
  3. We (eat) dinner when he arrived.
Show Answers1. She was reading a book at 8 pm. 2. They were not sleeping when I called. 3. We were eating dinner when he arrived.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When do we use the Past Continuous?

For actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

What is the difference between Past Continuous and Past Simple?

Past Continuous describes an ongoing action; Past Simple describes a completed action.

When do we use 'was' and 'were'?

Use 'was' for I/He/She/It, and 'were' for You/We/They.

Can I use 'yesterday' with Past Continuous?

Yes, if you specify a time: 'I was sleeping at 10 pm yesterday.'

What are common signal words?

While, when, at that time, as, at 8 pm, yesterday at 5 o'clock.
