\# Present Perfect Continuous Tense



\## Introduction



The Present Perfect Continuous Tense is used to describe actions that started in the past and are still continuing in the present, often with a focus on the duration. It is heavily used in IELTS Speaking and Writing to describe experiences and ongoing situations.



\*\*বাংলা:\*\*  

Present Perfect Continuous Tense অতীতে শুরু হওয়া এমন কাজ বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয় যা এখনও চলমান রয়েছে। এই Tense-এ কাজের সময়কাল (duration) গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।



\---



\## Learning Outcomes



\- Understand when to use the Present Perfect Continuous

\- Form sentences with have/has + been + verb-ing

\- Use 'for' and 'since' correctly

\- Distinguish between Present Perfect and Present Perfect Continuous



\---



\## When Do We Use It?



\- ✅ Actions that started in the past and continue to the present (e.g., \*I have been living here for 5 years.\*)

\- ✅ Actions that have just stopped but have a visible result (e.g., \*I am tired because I have been running.\*)

\- ✅ To emphasize how long something has been happening (e.g., \*She has been waiting for 2 hours.\*)
