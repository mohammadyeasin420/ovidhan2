# Past Continuous Tense

## What is the Past Continuous Tense?

The **Past Continuous Tense** is used for actions that were happening at a specific time in the past.

**বাংলা:**  
Past Continuous Tense অতীতে কোনো নির্দিষ্ট সময়ে চলমান কাজ বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

---

## Learning Outcomes

- Understand when to use the Past Continuous
- Form sentences with was/were + verb-ing
- Talk about actions in progress at a past time

---

## Formula

### Positive
**Subject + was/were + Verb-ing**  
Example: *She was reading a book.*

### Negative
**Subject + was/were + not + Verb-ing**  
Example: *They were not playing football.*

### Question
**Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?**  
Example: *Were you watching TV?*

---

## When Do We Use It?

- ✅ Actions in progress at a specific past time (e.g., *I was sleeping at 10 pm.*)
- ✅ Background actions interrupted by another event (e.g., *I was reading when the phone rang.*)
- ✅ Two simultaneous actions (e.g., *She was cooking while he was watching TV.*)

---

## 💡 Remember

Use **was** for I/He/She/It, and **were** for You/We/They.  
Always use **verb-ing**.
