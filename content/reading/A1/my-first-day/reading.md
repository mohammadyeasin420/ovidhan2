# My First Day at School

This is a short story for A1 learners. Read it carefully and answer the questions below.

---

## Vocabulary List

- **School** – স্কুল
- **Teacher** – শিক্ষক
- **Friend** – বন্ধু
- **Classroom** – শ্রেণিকক্ষ

---

## Comprehension Questions

**Who is the teacher?**
- Mrs. Brown - Mr. Smith - Ms. Jones

**How many friends does Ahmed make?**
- One - Two - Three

**Does Ahmed like his school?**
- Yes - No - Not sure
