\# Past Simple Tense



The Past Simple Tense is used to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past.



\## Rules

\- \*\*Positive:\*\* Subject + Verb 2 + Object

\- \*\*Negative:\*\* Subject + did not + Verb 1 + Object

\- \*\*Question:\*\* Did + Subject + Verb 1 + Object?



\## Examples

\- \*\*Positive:\*\* I visited Dhaka yesterday.

\- \*\*Negative:\*\* I did not visit Dhaka yesterday.

\- \*\*Question:\*\* Did you visit Dhaka yesterday?



\## Common Mistakes

\- ❌ I go to Dhaka yesterday. (Wrong)

\- ✅ I went to Dhaka yesterday. (Correct)



> Tip: Remember that "Did" already marks the past, so the main verb stays in its base form (Verb 1).
