During Meaning in Bangla

During এর বাংলা অর্থ চলাকালে / সময়ে। এর পরে সাধারণত noun phrase আসে, যেমন during the meeting।

English word
during
Bangla meaning
চলাকালে · সময়ে · কোনো সময়পর্বের মধ্যে
Part of speech
Preposition

Simple meaning

At some point within a period or event.

Through part or all of a particular period, depending on context.

বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

During একটি preposition। এটি বোঝায় যে কোনো ঘটনা একটি নির্দিষ্ট সময়, অনুষ্ঠান বা সময়পর্বের মধ্যে ঘটেছে। During-এর পরে সাধারণত noun বা noun phrase আসে: during the meeting, during the night। যদি subject + verb-সহ clause আসে, তখন সাধারণত while ব্যবহার করা হয়।

Examples

  1. I fell asleep during the movie.

    সিনেমা চলাকালে আমি ঘুমিয়ে পড়েছিলাম।

  2. She visited her parents during the summer.

    গ্রীষ্মকালে সে তার বাবা-মাকে দেখতে গিয়েছিল।

  3. During the meeting, we discussed the budget.

    মিটিং চলাকালে আমরা বাজেট নিয়ে আলোচনা করেছি।

  4. He injured his leg during the football match.

    ফুটবল ম্যাচ চলাকালে তার পায়ে আঘাত লাগে।

Usage note

  • during + noun/noun phrase: during the class, during Ramadan, during the trip.
  • Use while + subject + verb for a clause.
  • Use for + length of time to answer “how long?”

Common confusion

During vs. while — during + noun phrase; while + clause.

During vs. for — during identifies the period/event in which something happened; for states duration.

Common learner mistake

WRONG: I read a book during I was waiting. CORRECT: I read a book while I was waiting. WHY: I was waiting is a clause, so while is the natural choice.

Collocations and patterns

  • during the day
  • during the night
  • during the meeting
  • during the interview
  • during the holidays
  • during the trip

Word family

  • No direct word family; during is a preposition.

Quick practice

_____ the meeting, the manager announced a new rule.

Options: During / While / For

Check answer

Answer: During

the meeting একটি noun phrase, তাই during সঠিক।
