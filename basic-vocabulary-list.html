📖 Why Learn Basic Vocabulary?

Learning vocabulary is the foundation of English. If you know the right words, you can understand conversations, speak confidently, read books, write correctly, and pass exams like BCS, SSC, HSC, IELTS, and Bank Jobs.

💡 Tip: Focus on words you use every day – family, food, work, travel, health, and time. These appear in almost every conversation.

💡 টিপ: প্রতিদিন ব্যবহার করা শব্দগুলিতে ফোকাস করুন – পরিবার, খাবার, কাজ, ভ্রমণ, স্বাস্থ্য এবং সময়। এগুলি প্রায় প্রতিটি কথোপকথনে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

📅 Daily Vocabulary Plan

Don't try to learn hundreds of words in one day. Follow this simple routine:

Day 1
Learn 10 words from a category
Day 2
Practice pronunciation & examples
Day 3
Review with flashcards
Day 4
Take the category quiz
Day 5
Review all words from the week

💡 Tip: 10 words × 365 days = 3,650 new words in one year. Consistency beats intensity.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many English words should beginners learn?
Start with the most common 1,000–2,000 words. These cover a large portion of everyday conversations.
Should I memorize words every day?
Yes. Learning 10–15 new words daily and reviewing old ones consistently is more effective than studying hundreds at once.
Is this vocabulary useful for BCS and IELTS?
Yes. Basic vocabulary forms the foundation for all English exams, including BCS, Bank Jobs, SSC, HSC, and IELTS.
Can I learn without the internet?
Yes. Ovidhan is designed to support offline learning so you can study anytime.
