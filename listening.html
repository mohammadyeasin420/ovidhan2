How to practise listening effectively

Use each lesson as a short loop — not a one-time play-through.

প্রতিটি lesson একবার শুনে শেষ নয়। ছোট loop-এ বারবার শুনুন।

10–15 minute routine:
1) Listen once without pausing.
2) Read the Bangla support only for parts you missed.
3) Listen again and note 3–5 useful phrases.
4) Repeat key lines aloud (shadowing).
5) Try the lesson quiz if available.
Don’t translate every word
First catch the situation and the speaker’s goal.
Shadow short lines
Copy rhythm and stress — this helps speaking too.
Reuse vocabulary
Unknown words block listening; revise them the same day.

1) Everyday English (good first step)

Short social and daily situations. Best if you want confidence before exam listening.

  • Greeting a Neighbour
  • Buying Vegetables at the Market
  • Ordering Coffee
  • Talking About the Weather
  • Asking for Help

2) Travel & practical English

Airport, hotel, taxi, restaurant, and immigration-style conversations.

  • At the Airport (Check-in)
  • Ordering Food in a Restaurant
  • Taking a Taxi
  • At the Hotel (Check-in)
  • At the Immigration Desk

3) Workplace English

Office calls, meetings, updates, and interview introductions.

  • Job Interview Introduction
  • Making a Phone Call (Office)
  • Scheduling a Meeting
  • Giving a Status Update
  • Negotiating a Deadline

4) Academic / lecture-style listening

Longer talk and discussion practice before IELTS Section 3–4.

  • University Lecture (Introduction)
  • Academic Discussion (Group Work)
  • Listening to a Podcast (News)
  • Business News Report

5) IELTS listening practice

Use these after you are comfortable with everyday lessons. They follow the four IELTS listening section themes available on Ovidhan.

  • IELTS Listening Section 4 (Lecture)

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I improve English listening?
Practise a short lesson daily, shadow key lines, and revise the new phrases later the same day.
Is this listening practice free?
Yes. The lessons linked from this hub are free to use on Ovidhan.
Are Bangla explanations included?
Yes. Lesson pages include Bangla support to help you check meaning after the first listen.
Is this useful for IELTS?
Yes. Use the everyday lessons first, then move to the IELTS section lessons and mock test.
How long should I practise?
10–15 minutes a day is enough if you repeat and review.
