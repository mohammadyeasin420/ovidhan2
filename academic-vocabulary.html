1. Introduction: What is Academic Vocabulary?

Academic vocabulary is the set of words that are commonly used in textbooks, research papers, university lectures, and formal writing. These words are not always used in casual daily conversation, but they are essential for success in education and professional careers.

For Bangladeshi learners, mastering academic vocabulary opens doors to:

  • University Admission: DU, CU, NU, and private universities test academic English.
  • IELTS Band 7+: High-frequency academic words are essential for Writing and Speaking.
  • BCS & Bank Jobs: Competitive exams in Bangladesh heavily feature academic English.
  • Research & Study Abroad: Read and write research papers, assignments, and professional reports.

This hub is designed to teach you not just the words, but how to use them. We follow the Vocabulary Learning Ladder: Learn → Understand → Remember → Use → Master.

8. Common Academic Mistakes by Bangladeshi Learners

❌ Wrong: "I have did a research."
✅ Correct: "I have conducted research."
💡 Explanation: Use "conduct" or "carry out" with "research." "Did" is informal and incorrect in academic writing.
❌ Wrong: "The conclusion is very much important."
✅ Correct: "The conclusion is significant."
💡 Explanation: Use "significant" instead of "very much important" – it's more academic and concise.
❌ Wrong: "He told about the experiment."
✅ Correct: "He discussed the experiment."
💡 Explanation: "Discuss" is formal and appropriate for academic settings.
❌ Wrong: "The teacher gave us a lot of homeworks."
✅ Correct: "The teacher gave us a significant amount of homework."
💡 Explanation: "Homework" is uncountable. Use "significant amount" or "substantial."

11. Frequently Asked Questions

What is academic vocabulary? ▾
Academic vocabulary refers to words that are commonly used in educational settings, research papers, and formal writing. These include words like "analyze," "evaluate," "theory," and "evidence." They are essential for university study, IELTS, and competitive exams in Bangladesh.
How many academic words do I need to know? ▾
The Academic Word List (AWL) contains 570 essential word families. However, for practical purposes, mastering 300–500 key academic words will significantly improve your reading, writing, and speaking skills for exams and university.
What is the Academic Word List (AWL)? ▾
The Academic Word List (AWL) is a list of 570 word families that appear frequently in academic texts across many subjects. It was created by Averil Coxhead and is used worldwide by students preparing for university and exams like IELTS.
How can I learn academic vocabulary quickly? ▾
The best method is learning in context. Read academic texts, take notes, and practice using new words in your own sentences. Use flashcards with spaced repetition (available in the Dakho app) to review regularly.
Is this useful for IELTS? ▾
Yes. The IELTS Academic test requires a strong command of academic vocabulary for Writing Task 2, Speaking, and Reading. Our cluster pages include specific IELTS vocabulary sections.
