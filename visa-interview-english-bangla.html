🎙️ Example: Tourist Visa Interview Dialogue

Listen to the conversation and practice along.

💡 This is a sample dialogue for a tourist visa application. For student or work visas, the core phrases are similar, but your answers will differ.
👤 Interviewer:
Good morning. Please have a seat.
🧑‍🎓 You:
Good morning. Thank you.
👤 Interviewer:
Can I see your passport and application form?
🧑‍🎓 You:
Yes, here they are.
👤 Interviewer:
What is the purpose of your visit?
🧑‍🎓 You:
I am visiting the United States for tourism. I plan to visit New York and Washington D.C.
👤 Interviewer:
How long do you plan to stay?
🧑‍🎓 You:
I plan to stay for two weeks.
👤 Interviewer:
Do you have accommodation arranged?
🧑‍🎓 You:
Yes, I have booked a hotel. I have the confirmation with me.
👤 Interviewer:
Who will cover your expenses?
🧑‍🎓 You:
I will cover my own expenses. I have sufficient savings.
👤 Interviewer:
Thank you. Your application will be reviewed.
🧑‍🎓 You:
Thank you for your time.

🇧🇩 Bangla Explanation

Understand why we say these phrases in English.

Please have a seat."বসুন" – ভদ্রভাবে বসতে বলার উপায়।
What is the purpose of your visit?"আপনার ভ্রমণের উদ্দেশ্য কী?" – খুব সাধারণ ভিসা প্রশ্ন।
I plan to stay for two weeks."আমি দুই সপ্তাহ থাকার পরিকল্পনা করছি।" – "plan to" দিয়ে ভবিষ্যৎ পরিকল্পনা বোঝায়।
I have booked a hotel."আমি হোটেল বুক করেছি।" – Present Perfect দিয়ে সম্পন্ন কাজ বোঝানো।
I will cover my own expenses."নিজের ভ্রমণের খরচ নিজে বহন করলে এই বাক্যটি ব্যবহার করা যায়।" – ভাষাগত দিক থেকে সঠিক ও স্বাভাবিক।
I have been working for three years."আমি তিন বছর ধরে কাজ করছি।" – Present Perfect Continuous দিয়ে দীর্ঘমেয়াদি কাজ বোঝানো।

⚠️ Common Mistakes (Bangladeshi Learners)

Avoid these errors in your visa interview.

❌ Wrong: "I am agree to the terms."
✅ Correct: "I agree to the terms."
💡 Agree একটি verb, তাই "am" ব্যবহার করা হয় না।
❌ Wrong: "I want to tell about my purpose."
✅ Correct: "I want to talk about my purpose."
💡 "tell" সাধারণত একজন শ্রোতা/object নেয়: "Tell me about your job" ঠিক আছে, কিন্তু "tell about" ব্যবহার করলে object প্রয়োজন। "talk about" object ছাড়াই এই গঠনে ব্যবহার করা যায়।
✅ "I want to tell you about my job." (correct with object)
✅ "I want to talk about my job." (correct without object)
❌ Wrong: "I have done it yesterday."
✅ Correct: "I did it yesterday."
💡 "Yesterday" অতীত সময় নির্দেশ করে, তাই Past Simple ব্যবহার করতে হবে।
❌ Wrong: "My all documents are ready."
✅ Correct: "All my documents are ready."
💡 "My all" ভুল; "All my" সঠিক।
