Formal Letter Writing in Bangla – Letter to the Editor

বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা, স্বাভাবিক English model, BPSC-এর official task, ছয়টি practice এবং একটি deterministic concept quizসহ formal letter লেখার পূর্ণ lesson।

📅 August 21, 2026⏱ 30 min read✍️ Mohammad YeasinSkill: Formal Letter Writing

📌 কী শিখবেন?

Formal letter হলো পেশাগত, প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক বা জনসংশ্লিষ্ট উদ্দেশ্যে সংযত ও সুসংগঠিত ভাষায় লেখা চিঠি। Letter to the Editor এই বৃহত্তর skill-এর একটি প্রয়োগ—এতে কোনো public বা thematic issue নিয়ে concern, opinion, response, recommendation বা awareness-এর কথা বলা যায়।

Formal letter skill

  • উদ্দেশ্য ও প্রাপক বোঝা
  • স্পষ্ট structure
  • পেশাদার tone
  • প্রাসঙ্গিক request বা recommendation

Letter to the Editor

  • সম্পাদককে লেখা
  • জনস্বার্থ বা thematic issue
  • যুক্তিসংগত concern/opinion
  • গঠনমূলক response বা recommendation

🏗️ একটি ব্যবহারযোগ্য formal-letter structure

Ovidhan teaching convention: নিচের layout একটি প্রচলিত ও পরীক্ষায় ব্যবহারযোগ্য convention। এটি BPSC-এর নির্ধারিত একমাত্র official format নয়; official syllabus task ও marks বলে, কিন্তু একটিমাত্র বাধ্যতামূলক layout নির্ধারণ করে না। প্রশ্নে আলাদা নির্দেশনা থাকলে সেটিই অনুসরণ করুন।

১. Sender details ও date

প্রাসঙ্গিক হলে sender address, তারপর date।

২. Receiver details

প্রাসঙ্গিক হলে designation ও organization।

৩. Subject

এক লাইনে letter-এর মূল purpose।

৪. Salutation

এই task-এ Dear Editor, একটি স্পষ্ট gender-neutral option। Dear Sir or Madam, নিজে থেকে ভুল নয়।

৫. Opening

কেন লিখছেন তা সরাসরি ও ভদ্রভাবে বলুন।

৬. Body

Issue → significance/evidence → response বা recommendation।

৭. Close ও name

সংক্ষিপ্ত final request, complimentary close এবং name।

Sender's details (if appropriate) Date The Editor / Receiver's designation Organization Subject: Clear purpose of the letter Dear Editor, Opening: purpose and context Body: concern/opinion, significance, and relevant support Closing: constructive request or recommendation Yours sincerely/faithfully, Name

I want you to publish this.I would be grateful if you would consider highlighting this issue.I want সব সময় informal নয়, তবে formal request-এ এটি অতিরিক্ত direct শোনাতে পারে।
People are suffering very muchly.Many residents are experiencing serious hardship.বাংলা কাঠামো সরাসরি অনুবাদ না করে natural collocation ব্যবহার করুন।
Everybody knows this terrible disaster.This issue has affected many residents.অতিরঞ্জন বাদ দিয়ে factual neutrality রাখুন।

  • Emotional intensity-এর বদলে precise impact লিখুন।
  • এক বাক্যে এক প্রধান idea রাখুন।
  • বাংলা থেকে word-for-word translation এড়িয়ে natural English phrase ব্যবহার করুন।
  • অভিযোগের সঙ্গে বাস্তবসম্মত recommendation দিন।

🏛️ BPSC: official requirement বনাম teaching convention

Official BPSC requirement

বর্তমান official syllabus অনুযায়ী English (Compulsory), Subject Code 003-এর মোট নম্বর 200; Part A-এর নম্বর 100। এই অংশের 20-mark letter task-এ candidate-কে given passage-এর thematic issue-এর সঙ্গে সম্পর্কিত একটি letter কোনো English newspaper-এর editor-কে লিখতে হয়।

Official BPSC syllabus download page · Officially hosted syllabus PDF

Ovidhan teaching convention

এই page একটি common formal-letter layout শেখায় যাতে candidate পরিষ্কারভাবে উত্তর সাজাতে পারে। Syllabus sender/receiver address বা একটি exact layout-কে mandatory বলে না। বর্তমান official syllabus এই task-এর জন্য fixed word limit-ও দেয় না। প্রশ্নের instruction অনুসরণ করুন এবং concisely ও relevantly লিখুন।

৭ ধাপে BPSC-style response

  1. Passage-এর thematic issue এক বাক্যে লিখুন।
  2. Prompt-এর audience ও action word শনাক্ত করুন।
  3. নিজের central concern/opinion ঠিক করুন।
  4. দুই বা তিনটি relevant supporting point নিন।
  5. একটি constructive recommendation বা response দিন।
  6. Common convention মেনে coherent draft লিখুন।
  7. Relevance, tone, grammar ও question instruction যাচাই করুন।

✍️ তিনটি worked example

Provenance: নিচের worked examples এবং পরের practice prompts Ovidhan-created exam-style practice—এগুলো official past BCS question নয়।
WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · GENERAL FORMAL LETTER

Library opening hours

Prompt: Write to the college librarian requesting extended library hours before examinations.

21 August 2026 The Librarian City College, Dhaka Subject: Request for Extended Library Hours Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing on behalf of several students to request extended library hours during the examination period. Many students depend on the library for reference books and a quiet study environment, but the current closing time limits access after regular classes. I respectfully request that the library remain open for two additional hours on weekdays during the examination month. A temporary schedule would support students without requiring a permanent change. Thank you for considering this request. Yours faithfully, Nabila Rahman

কেন কাজ করে: নির্দিষ্ট audience, practical reason এবং proportionate request আছে; tone respectful।

WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · LETTER TO THE EDITOR

Pedestrian safety

Prompt: Write to the editor about pedestrian safety near schools.

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Improving Pedestrian Safety Near Schools Dear Editor, I am writing to draw attention to unsafe road conditions near many urban schools. Students often cross busy roads without working signals, marked crossings, or adequate supervision. The risk is particularly serious during opening and closing hours. Local authorities could improve safety by installing visible crossings, enforcing lower speed limits, and assigning trained traffic personnel at peak times. Schools can also provide age-appropriate road-safety guidance. I hope your newspaper will help raise informed public awareness of this preventable risk. Yours sincerely, Arif Hasan

কেন কাজ করে: Issue, impact, recommendation এবং editor-এর কাছে reasonable public-awareness request—সবই আছে।

WORKED EXAMPLE 3 · BPSC-STYLE

Responsible use of social media

Thematic passage: Social media improves connection and access to information, but misinformation, privacy risks, and unhealthy use require responsible responses.

Prompt: Write a letter relating to the thematic issue of the passage to the editor of an English newspaper. (20 marks)

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Promoting Responsible Use of Social Media Dear Editor, I am writing in response to the important issue of responsible social-media use. Digital platforms provide quick access to information and allow people to communicate across distances. However, unverified claims, privacy breaches, and excessive use can harm both individuals and communities. Users should verify information before sharing it and review their privacy settings regularly. Schools and public institutions can strengthen media-literacy education, while platforms should respond more transparently to harmful content. Your newspaper can contribute by publishing practical guidance that encourages informed and balanced digital habits. Yours sincerely, Samira Ahmed

কেন কাজ করে: Passage-এর সুবিধা ও risk উভয়ের সঙ্গে সরাসরি connection আছে এবং balanced recommendation দেওয়া হয়েছে।

⚠️ Common mistakes

এড়িয়ে চলুন

  • একটি exact format-কে official দাবি করা
  • Passage-এর thematic issue বাদ দেওয়া
  • Overly direct demand বা personal attack
  • Unsupported statistics বা political advocacy
  • Memorized introduction যা prompt-এর সঙ্গে মেলে না

এর বদলে করুন

  • Question instruction আগে পড়ুন
  • Issue-এর সঙ্গে প্রতিটি paragraph যুক্ত করুন
  • Neutral, precise wording ব্যবহার করুন
  • Realistic recommendation দিন
  • শেষে relevance ও grammar check করুন

Practice 1 — Beginner

Prompt: Write to your college principal requesting a cleaner drinking-water area.

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Principal City College, Dhaka Subject: Request for a Cleaner Drinking-Water Area Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to request regular cleaning of the drinking-water area on our campus. The floor often remains wet, and the waste bin is not emptied frequently. This creates discomfort and may affect hygiene. I respectfully request a daily cleaning schedule and a covered waste bin. Clear signs could also encourage students to keep the area tidy. Yours faithfully, Rafi Ahmed

Self-check

  • Purpose clear?
  • Tone respectful?
  • Request practical?
  • Grammar checked?

Practice 2 — Beginner

Prompt: Write to a local newspaper editor about littering in a public park.

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Keeping Public Parks Free from Litter Dear Editor, I am writing to express concern about increasing litter in a public park in our area. Plastic packaging and food waste make the park unpleasant and may block nearby drains. The local authority could provide covered bins, arrange regular collection, and display clear reminders. Visitors must also dispose of waste responsibly. I hope your newspaper will encourage shared responsibility for public spaces. Yours sincerely, Maliha Karim

Self-check

  • Public issue identified?
  • Recommendation included?
  • No exaggerated claim?
  • Closing appropriate?

Practice 3 — Intermediate

Prompt: Write to a transport authority suggesting clearer passenger information during service disruptions.

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Customer Service Manager City Transport Authority Dhaka Subject: Clearer Information During Service Disruptions Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to suggest improvements to passenger information when services are delayed or diverted. Commuters often receive little notice and may wait without knowing the expected delay or an alternative route. Real-time notices at stops, concise website updates, and trained staff at major stations would help passengers make informed decisions. I would be grateful if the authority considered a consistent disruption-information policy. Yours faithfully, Tania Noor

Self-check

  • Audience appropriate?
  • Problem explained?
  • Suggestions specific?
  • Tone professional?

Practice 4 — Intermediate

Prompt: Write to the editor offering an opinion and recommendations on excessive noise near hospitals.

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Reducing Excessive Noise Near Hospitals Dear Editor, I am writing to highlight excessive vehicle horns and amplified sound near several hospitals. A quieter environment is important for patients, attendants, and medical staff, yet existing restrictions are often ignored. Clear quiet-zone signs, consistent enforcement, and public-information campaigns could reduce unnecessary noise. Transport operators should also train drivers to respect hospital zones. Your newspaper can help explain why responsible behaviour matters in these areas. Yours sincerely, Imran Chowdhury

Self-check

  • Concern factual and neutral?
  • Recommendations realistic?
  • Editor role reasonable?
  • Language concise?

Practice 5 — Exam-style

Thematic passage: Online learning expands access and flexibility, but unequal access to reliable devices and internet connections can leave some learners behind.

Prompt: Write a letter relating to the thematic issue of the passage to the editor of an English newspaper. (20 marks)

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Making Online Learning More Inclusive Dear Editor, I am writing about unequal access to online learning. Digital classes can make education flexible and extend useful resources beyond the classroom. However, students without reliable devices, affordable data, or stable connections cannot benefit equally. Public institutions could expand low-cost connectivity, provide shared learning facilities, and make essential materials available in formats that work with limited bandwidth. Schools should also retain reasonable offline alternatives where necessary. I hope your newspaper will encourage a practical discussion about inclusive digital education. Yours sincerely, Nusrat Jahan

Self-check

  • Thematic connection explicit?
  • Benefits and challenge balanced?
  • Recommendation relevant?
  • Question instructions followed?

Practice 6 — Exam-style

Thematic passage: Rapid urbanization creates employment and economic opportunity, but overcrowding, pollution, inadequate housing, and shrinking green spaces can reduce urban quality of life.

Prompt: Write a letter relating to the thematic issue of the passage to the editor of an English newspaper. (20 marks)

Model answer

21 August 2026 The Editor An English Daily Dhaka Subject: Planning for Livable and Sustainable Cities Dear Editor, I am writing about the challenges associated with rapid urbanization. Cities create employment, services, and economic opportunity, but unmanaged growth can intensify overcrowding, pollution, housing pressure, and the loss of green space. Long-term planning should coordinate public transport, affordable housing, drainage, and environmental protection. Local authorities should publish measurable plans and consult affected communities before major development decisions. Your newspaper can support an informed discussion that recognizes both the benefits of urban growth and the need for responsible planning. Yours sincerely, Farhana Islam

Self-check

  • Issue connected to passage?
  • Position balanced?
  • Recommendations constructive?
  • Tone and grammar checked?

❓ Frequently asked questions

English (Compulsory), Subject Code 003-এর Part A-তে given passage-এর thematic issue সম্পর্কিত একটি letter English newspaper-এর editor-কে লিখতে হয়। এতে 20 marks বরাদ্দ। এটি বর্তমান syllabus-এর wording; এখানে universal historical claim করা হচ্ছে না।

বর্তমান syllabus task ও marks নির্ধারণ করে, কিন্তু sender/receiver addressসহ একটি exact layout mandatory বলে না। এই page-এর layout একটি common teaching convention।

বর্তমান official BPSC syllabus fixed word limit দেয় না। Question instruction অনুসরণ করুন এবং concisely ও relevantly লিখুন।

না। এটি inherently incorrect নয়। তবে editor-কে লেখা এই নির্দিষ্ট task-এ “Dear Editor,” একটি clear এবং gender-neutral option।

Phrase-টি intrinsically informal নয়, কিন্তু formal request-এ “I want you to...” অতিরিক্ত direct শোনাতে পারে। Context অনুযায়ী “I would like to request...” বা অন্য respectful construction বেশি উপযোগী।

না। এটি public বা thematic issue নিয়ে concern, opinion, response, recommendation বা awareness প্রকাশ করতে পারে। BPSC task-এ passage-এর thematic issue-এর সঙ্গে connection জরুরি।

না। Textarea-এর লেখা browser tab-এর memory-তে থাকে; page reload বা tab close করলে হারিয়ে যায়। এটি persist, transmit বা automatically grade করা হয় না।
