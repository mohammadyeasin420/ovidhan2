1. What Are Academic Verbs?

Academic verbs are the backbone of formal writing. They are the verbs you use in essays, research papers, university assignments, and IELTS Writing Task 2.

Unlike everyday verbs like "get," "put," or "make," academic verbs are more precise, formal, and powerful. For example, instead of saying "The results show...", you would say "The results demonstrate..." or "The results indicate..."

This guide provides a comprehensive academic verbs list with Bangla meanings, pronunciation, and practical examples for Bangladeshi learners preparing for:

  • IELTS Academic (Writing Task 1 & 2, Speaking)
  • University admission (DU, CU, NU, private universities)
  • BCS & Bank job exams
  • Research writing and academic publications

3. Analysis Verbs (বিশ্লেষণমূলক ক্রিয়া)

Use these verbs to break down ideas, data, or arguments into parts.

Analyze /ˈænəlaɪz/ বিশ্লেষণ করা "Rahim analyzed the BCS question pattern." ★★★★★ (Essential)
Examine /ɪɡˈzæmɪn/ পরীক্ষা করা "The researcher examined the data carefully." ★★★★★ (Essential)
Investigate /ɪnˈvestɪɡeɪt/ তদন্ত করা "Scientists investigate the causes of climate change." ★★★★☆ (Very Useful)
Explore /ɪkˈsplɔːr/ অন্বেষণ করা "The study explores the impact of social media." ★★★★☆ (Very Useful)
Scrutinize /ˈskruːtɪnaɪz/ যাচাই করা "The committee scrutinized the budget proposal." ★★★☆☆ (Useful)
Inspect /ɪnˈspekt/ পরিদর্শন করা "The auditor inspected the financial records." ★★★☆☆ (Useful)

13. Common Mistakes with Academic Verbs (Bangladesh Focus)

❌ Wrong: "I have did a research."
✅ Correct: "I have conducted research."
💡 Explanation: Use "conduct" or "carry out" with "research." "Did" is informal and incorrect in academic writing.
❌ Wrong: "The teacher gave us a lot of homeworks."
✅ Correct: "The teacher assigned a significant amount of homework."
💡 Explanation: "Homework" is uncountable. Use "assigned" instead of "gave" and "significant amount" instead of "a lot of."
❌ Wrong: "He told about the experiment."
✅ Correct: "He discussed the experiment."
💡 Explanation: "Discuss" is formal and appropriate for academic settings. "Tell" is informal.

15. Frequently Asked Questions

What are academic verbs? ▾
Academic verbs are formal verbs used in research papers, university essays, and IELTS Writing. Examples include analyze, evaluate, demonstrate, and establish. They are more precise than everyday verbs.
Why are academic verbs important for IELTS? ▾
IELTS Writing Task 1 and 2 require a range of formal verbs. Using 'indicate' instead of 'show' or 'demonstrate' instead of 'prove' helps you achieve a higher Lexical Resource score (Band 7+).
What is the difference between academic verbs and normal verbs? ▾
Academic verbs are more formal and precise. For example, instead of 'get,' you use 'obtain' or 'acquire.' Instead of 'look at,' you use 'examine' or 'analyze.'
What are reporting verbs? ▾
Reporting verbs are used to cite or refer to what others have said or written. Examples include 'state,' 'argue,' 'suggest,' 'claim,' and 'reveal.' They are essential for academic writing and IELTS essays.
Are academic verbs useful for BCS and Bank exams? ▾
Yes. BCS Preliminary, Bank Written, and other competitive exams in Bangladesh frequently test synonyms, antonyms, and sentence correction. Academic verbs are often the correct formal choice.
Can I use academic verbs in IELTS Speaking? ▾
Yes, but sparingly. IELTS Speaking is semi-formal. Using academic verbs like 'analyze' or 'evaluate' appropriately in Part 3 can impress the examiner. However, avoid them in Part 1 (Personal Introduction).
What are the most common academic verbs? ▾
The most frequent academic verbs include analyze, evaluate, demonstrate, establish, indicate, identify, assess, examine, investigate, and suggest. These appear in over 50% of academic texts.
How many academic verbs should I memorize? ▾
Start with 60–80 common academic verbs. Mastering this core set covers 80% of what you need for university essays, research papers, and IELTS Writing. Our list above is a great starting point.
What is the difference between academic verbs and action verbs? ▾
Action verbs describe physical or mental actions (e.g., run, eat, think). Academic verbs are a subset of action verbs that are used specifically in formal, educational, and professional contexts (e.g., evaluate, hypothesize).
What verbs improve IELTS writing the most? ▾
For IELTS Writing Task 1 (Report), use 'indicate,' 'demonstrate,' 'illustrate,' and 'fluctuate.' For Task 2 (Essay), use 'argue,' 'claim,' 'suggest,' 'emphasize,' and 'conclude.'
Where can I practice academic verbs? ▾
You can practice with flashcards, quizzes, and exercises in the Dakho app (free download below). You can also write practice essays using these verbs and check them with our grammar checker.
Are academic verbs used in research papers? ▾
Yes, absolutely. Research papers rely heavily on academic verbs to describe methodology, results, and conclusions. Examples include 'investigate,' 'hypothesize,' 'measure,' 'quantify,' and 'substantiate.'
