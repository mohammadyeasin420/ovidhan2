BCS English

For BCS Preliminary and Written candidates who need high-frequency vocabulary, grammar accuracy, synonyms/antonyms, and pattern familiarity.

BCS পরীক্ষার্থীদের জন্য: বারবার আসা vocabulary, grammar rules, synonym-antonym এবং question pattern — এখান থেকে শুরু করুন।

What to focus on

Vocabulary recognition, grammar MCQs, synonyms/antonyms, and recent question patterns matter more than long essays at the Preliminary stage.

IELTS Preparation

For students aiming to study or migrate abroad who need band-focused strategy plus vocabulary and listening support.

বিদেশে পড়াশোনা বা মাইগ্রেশনের জন্য IELTS প্রস্তুতি: strategy, vocabulary এবং listening practice একসাথে ব্যবহার করুন।

Understand each module’s task type, build topic vocabulary, and practise listening with real lesson pages — not only word lists.

Bank Job English

For candidates preparing for Bangladesh bank recruitment written exams and related professional English.

Banking/finance vocabulary, accurate grammar, and clear written English used in letters, paragraphs, and MCQs.
